How a day actually runs

  1. 07:30 — Weather call

    Your leader sends the real forecast reading: wind on the tops, a go/no-go, and what to wear. No call, no walk — you are never left guessing.

  2. 09:00 — Meet & brief

    Fifteen minutes on the route, the map and the plan B. Packs are checked; water and a shared flask are standard kit.

  3. Midday — Long lunch

    A proper stop, not a sandwich in the wind. Geology, birds and local history come out when the group wants them.

  4. 17:00 — Debrief

    Back at the car with a route log, a photo drop link and honest notes on what the next step up would look like.
