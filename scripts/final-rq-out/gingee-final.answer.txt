As of October 27, 2026, a seven-year-old girl from Tamil Nadu who went missing near Gingee remains untraced 15 days after her disappearance, prompting a CB-CID investigation and public outrage [3].

### Confirmed timeline:
- October 12, 2026: The seven-year-old girl went missing from her home near Gingee [2].
- October 16, 2026: Police intensified search operations for the missing girl [2].
- October 22, 2026: Extensive searches failed to yield results, and the CB-CID officially began its probe into the case [1].
- October 25-26, 2026: The CB-CID continued the investigation, with the DGP conducting daily reviews of the search operation [3].

### Latest verified update:
As of October 27, 2026, India Today reported that the girl remained untraced 15 days after her disappearance, with over 100 police personnel involved in the search operation [3]. Minister Nirmal Kumar stated that police are probing all angles of the case [4].

### Unresolved questions:
The retrieved evidence does not specify the exact circumstances of the disappearance or any identified suspects. The case continues to be under active investigation by the CB-CID, with no confirmed updates on the girl's whereabouts or the progress of the investigation beyond October 27, 2026.
